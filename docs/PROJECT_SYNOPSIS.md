# Project Synopsis: Multi-Agent Research and Report Factory

## 1. Problem Statement
In academic, scientific, and enterprise intelligence gathering, synthesizing comprehensive, verifiable reports on complex topics requires navigating hundreds of heterogeneous sources, isolating quantitative data from corporate marketing hyperbole, cross-checking conflicting claims, and adhering to strict citation rigor. Single-prompt Large Language Models (LLMs) or rigid, linear automation workflows fail at this task: single prompts suffer from hallucinations and context saturation, while linear pipelines execute blindly without evaluating evidence quality, missing critical knowledge gaps, and failing to adjust search strategies dynamically.

## 2. Our Multi-Agent Solution
The **Multi-Agent Research and Report Factory** is an autonomous, adaptive multi-agent system consisting of five specialized AI agents: **Strategic Planner**, **Literature Scout (Searcher)**, **Deep Reader**, **Epistemic Critic**, and **Executive Synthesizer (Writer)**. Unlike traditional scripted assembly lines, our architecture employs an **Adaptive Orchestrator-Worker with Evaluator-Optimizer Critique Loop** pattern:
- The **Planner** deconstructs research taxonomy, sub-questions, and multi-perspective hypotheses.
- The **Searcher** and **Reader** autonomously probe web engines, academic repositories, and internal document vaults to extract structured claims with exact provenance.
- The **Critic** acts as the cognitive auditor, evaluating domain authority, calculating an Epistemic Reliability Index (0–100), running adversarial Red Team stress-tests, and detecting knowledge gaps.
- **Dynamic Replanning & Feedback Loops**: If the Critic identifies evidence deficits or conflicting claims, it dynamically formulates follow-up queries and dispatches the Searcher and Reader on supplementary investigation loops. Furthermore, the Critic performs a post-draft anti-hallucination audit on the Writer's draft, demanding targeted revisions before final sign-off.
- The **Writer** synthesizes the hardened evidence into an exhaustive whitepaper with standard footnote citations, interactive charts, slide decks, and a two-speaker audio briefing.

## 3. Target User & Impact
The platform targets executive strategists, technical researchers, investment analysts, and academic scholars who require audit-grade, cited intelligence. By visualizing real-time thought streams, inter-agent handoffs, and feedback loops on an interactive dashboard, the system provides transparent, trustworthy research operations.
